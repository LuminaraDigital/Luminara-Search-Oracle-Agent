# Deslop

Adapted from Every/OpenSEO deslop (MIT). Strip predictable AI patterns from writing.

## Rules

1. Cut filler ("Here's the thing", "Let that sink in", "In this section we'll explore").
2. Avoid binary contrasts ("Not X. Y."), dramatic fragments, and rhetorical "The result?" patterns.
3. Drop tropes: delve, tapestry, nuanced (as filler), "serves as", false ranges, stakes inflation.
4. Prefer active voice with named actors.
5. Be specific: numbers, URLs, quoted evidence. No vague "experts argue".
6. Vary sentence rhythm. No em dashes. Two items beat three.
7. Trust readers: no "Let's break this down" or fractal summaries.
8. No bold-first bullet walls unless a skill explicitly requires short labels.

When writing luminara-report HTML, prefer Status / Fix / Why bullets over essays.
