What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the work of making a page easy for an answer engine to quote. That includes Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity. AEO is not a score.

Luminara Suite audits how a website shows up in those answers, then ranks one next fix. The product is a web app, a Windows desktop app, and a Telegram Mini App from Luminara Digital.

How Suite measures it

Instant Audit fetches a public page and a search sample when the hosted rail or the caller's keys allow it. Citation rate, share of voice, and page health appear only when that run collected live evidence.

LLM crawler checks are separate. They read robots.txt, llms.txt, and optional ai.txt. Each row is pass, fail, or not measured:

  • llms.txt presence
  • llms.txt structure: a title and a short summary. This is a hint, not a score.
  • Common AI crawler directives in robots.txt
  • Whether those crawlers are blocked from the homepage, /llms.txt, or /sitemap.xml
  • Optional ai.txt. A missing file is recorded. It is not a citation score.

None of these rows is a 0-100 score, and none of them changes the page health figure.

Agents may cite redacted teaser cards under /share/teaser/. robots.txt allows that path for named AI crawlers and for every other user-agent. Full branded reports under /share/ stay disallowed, along with /api/, /verify/, /reports/, and /oauth/. A teaser repeats a short verdict and one next move. It is not a verified measurement.

Honesty glossary

  • measured: the figure comes from evidence collected in that run.
  • estimated: a labelled estimate, never presented as a measured score.
  • not_measured: the provider failed, the sample was empty, or the signal was not collected.

Suite does not invent citation percentages, health scores, or share of voice when evidence is missing.

What may an agent cite?

Agents may cite public marketing pages, /llms.txt, this page, and redacted cards under /share/teaser/. Full branded reports under /share/ stay disallowed, along with /api/, /verify/, /reports/, and /oauth/. A teaser is a redacted summary, not a verified measurement.

Does llms.txt guarantee a citation?

No. Google Search may ignore llms.txt. Other agents can still quote it. Suite does not invent a citation percentage when evidence is missing.
