Luminara MCP

Connect Cursor, Claude, or Codex to https://luminarasuite.com/api/mcp with a Growth or Agency API key (Authorization: Bearer lm_live_...).

MCP is on Growth and Agency. Free has no IDE access. On Growth and Agency, the included tools are projects, context, and reports. Paid research needs Agency apiAccess or your own DataForSEO key (x-provider-key).

Plugin and skills live in the repo under plugins/luminara/.
