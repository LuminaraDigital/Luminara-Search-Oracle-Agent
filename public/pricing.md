# Plans for audits, share links, and reminders.

For independent real estate agencies in Australia, Oceania and South-East Asia. Free covers Sample scouts and a daily hosted Instant Audit allowance after you create an account. Starter is web audits only. Growth adds IDE access and shareable reports. Agency adds API access and client workspaces. Live answers from specific AI products stay Not measured unless you are on Agency or you connect your own search login.

Each paid plan lasts 30 days and starts as soon as payment is confirmed. It does not renew on its own. Starter A$79, Growth A$229, Agency A$549. Pay by card for a 30-day pass that does not renew, or pay in Telegram Stars in the Mini App.
