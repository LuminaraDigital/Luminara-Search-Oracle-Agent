2. Scope

This policy applies to web visitors, signed-in users (via Firebase Google/email authentication or Telegram initData), Telegram Stars / TON subscribers, and users interacting with the Telegram Bot or Mini App. Self-hosted deployments operated independently remain under their operator's own privacy obligations.

3. Data We Process

Categories of personal data depend on how you interact with Luminara Suite:

  • Account Identifiers: In Telegram, we receive cryptographic signed launch data (initData) containing your Telegram user ID, first name, last name, username, language code, and optional profile photo URL. On web, Firebase UID, email address, and display name. We may link Telegram and Firebase identities under a unified account ID.
  • Business Content & Audit Queries: Business DNA inputs, domains, competitor brand names, chat messages, audit queries, Brand Memory vectors, and client workspaces you configure.
  • BYOK Credentials: API keys you paste (Groq, NVIDIA NIM, Google Gemini, Tavily, Firecrawl, Exa, OpenRouter, Ollama) are stored in client-side storage. If you sign in with workspace sync enabled, keys may be securely encrypted in your server-side workspace record (Cloudflare D1/KV). You may choose local-only storage at any time.
  • Usage & Subscriptions: Plan tier, expiration timestamp, Telegram Stars receipt IDs, TON payment order memos and transaction hashes, daily free-quota counters, and Sentinel alert targets (domain, chat ID, scores).
  • Technical Logs: Cloudflare edge telemetry, IP addresses utilized in-isolate for transient abuse rate limiting, and security logs. We do not set third-party marketing or cross-site tracking cookies.

4. Where Data Lives & Security Architecture

  • On Your Device: localStorage and sessionStorage hold your Business DNA, active chat sessions, audit reports, and local keys.
  • On Cloudflare Edge: Cloudflare D1 (account mappings and workspace data) and Cloudflare KV (subscriptions, daily rate limits, and Sentinel targets) located globally at Cloudflare edge data centers with HTTPS encryption in transit.
  • Firebase Authentication: Google Firebase handles email and Google Sign-In credentials under Google Cloud security protocols.

5. Purposes & Legal Bases

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Providing search visibility audits, Oracle Agent chat, Brand Memory, and Drift Sentinel monitoring.
  • Authenticating your session, metering free daily quotas, and activating Telegram Stars / TON plans.
  • Relaying browser AI calls through secure Cloudflare Worker proxies without server persistence.
  • Defending against abuse, denial-of-service attacks, and malicious credential extraction.
  • Fulfilling customer support, billing assistance, and privacy inquiries.

Legal bases under GDPR/UK GDPR: Performance of contract to deliver the services requested; legitimate interests in ensuring security and uptime; consent when connecting optional third-party wallets or provider keys; and legal compliance regarding accounting and dispute resolution.

6. AI & Search Sub-Processors

When you execute an audit or conversational inquiry, domain URLs, scraped text extracts, and prompts are dispatched to model and search providers:

  • LLM Inference: Groq Cloud, NVIDIA NIM, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or your configured Ollama host.
  • Search & Crawling: Tavily, Exa, Firecrawl.
  • Infrastructure: Cloudflare Inc. (Edge compute, D1, KV), Google Cloud Platform / Firebase.
  • Telegram & TON: Telegram FZ-LLC (Mini App platform, Stars billing, Bot API) and The Open Network (decentralized on-chain payment verification).

7. Telegram Bot & Wallet Specifics

When using the bot @LuminaraSuiteBot or launching the Mini App:

  • We authenticate your request using Telegram's HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.
  • We never receive or store your payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or phone number. Telegram Stars payments are handled entirely by Telegram.
  • Connecting a TON wallet exposes only your public wallet address to facilitate payment verification; we never access, request, or store private keys or seed phrases.
  • You can reset conversation history with the bot at any time by sending /reset or /clear.

8. Data Retention & Erasure

  • Browser Data: Retained until you clear browser site data or reset keys in Settings.
  • Daily Quota Counters: Expire automatically within 48 hours (KV TTL).
  • Bot Chat Context: Retained temporarily in KV to support conversation continuity, erasable via /reset.
  • Account & Subscriptions: Retained while your account remains active. Verified deletion requests to privacy@luminarasuite.com are fulfilled within 30 days, subject to mandatory legal retention and immutable public blockchain records.

9. Your Rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA, you have rights to access, rectify, port, and erase your personal data, or object to certain processing:

  • To request data export or server-side erasure, email privacy@luminarasuite.com with your Telegram username/ID or registered email.
  • To inspect active subscription status in Telegram, send /status to the bot.
  • To clear local data immediately, use the in-app Settings to clear API keys and reset local cache.
  • We do not sell personal data for monetary or other valuable consideration.
