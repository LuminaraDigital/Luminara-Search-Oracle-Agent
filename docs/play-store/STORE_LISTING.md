## Full Description (4000 chars max - 1,890 chars used)

```text
Luminara Suite is an evidence-bound SEO, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) workbench for website operators, technical marketers, and engineering teams.

Verify how search engines and conversational AI crawlers read your domain in real time, without fabricated scores or black-box estimates.

KEY CAPABILITIES

1. Live Instant Audit (No Sign-In Required)
- Inspect live /robots.txt, /llms.txt, and /ai.txt files on any public domain.
- Evaluate 5 core LLM crawler readiness checks: llms.txt presence, markdown structure, AI bot directives, canonical citation links, and ai.txt policy headers.
- Audit your 10-bot AI Governance Matrix across GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, and Bytespider.
- Verify deployed fixes live with one-tap check re-runs.

2. Ask the Search Oracle
- Query an evidence-bound SEO, AEO, and GEO advisor grounded in live web search results.
- Receive a concise verdict, one prioritized next action, and cited web sources for every question.
- Includes built-in AI output reporting controls.

3. Offline-First Operator Dashboard
- Every audit and technical finding is cached locally on your device using Room 3 SQLite so you can review history and update action-board items (Open, In Progress, Done) even when offline.
- Syncs with your Luminara cloud workspace when signed in.

4. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) or Use Hosted Checks
- Sign in with your Luminara account for cloud workspace sync and 3 hosted AI checks per UTC day.
- Or bring your own API keys (Groq, Tavily, Google Gemini, NVIDIA NIM) in Settings. BYOK keys are encrypted locally using Android Keystore AES-256-GCM.

HONESTY & PRIVACY BY DESIGN
- Unmeasured signals are explicitly labeled NOT_MEASURED rather than estimated.
- Encrypted in transit over HTTPS/TLS.
- Self-serve JSON data export and permanent in-app account deletion in Settings.

Operator: Luminara Digital Pty Ltd (ABN 90 693 125 281), Melbourne, VIC, Australia.
```
